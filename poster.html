THURSDAY  ·  ONE DAY'S WORLD NEWS, IN ONE PICTURE
1
TRADE  ·  WASHINGTON  ·  ①

US PROPOSES NEW TARIFFS ON 14 PARTNERS

The Trump administration proposed new tariffs on 14 major trading partners after an eight-month probe into “structural excess capacity”. China faces 20% — 40% if the US–China truce lapses — the EU 15% and India 17–25%. A public hearing is set for 25 January.

2
THANKSGIVING  ·  NEW YORK

STORM GROUNDS THE 100TH MACY'S PARADE

The 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, due today, was postponed on Wednesday to Saturday 28 Nov, 8:30am ET, as a nor'easter brings gusts up to ~97 km/h. It is the first postponement in the parade's history.

3
MARKETS  ·  NEW YORK

NASDAQ SLIDES AGAIN ON TARIFFS AND AI FEARS

US stocks fell further in early trading as AI valuation fears intensified and the tariff announcement spooked markets: the Nasdaq was down almost 4%, with all three main indexes down.

4
DISASTER  ·  CAIRO

31 KILLED IN CAIRO RING ROAD FIRE

A fuel tanker crashed and overturned on Cairo's Ring Road in Al-Warraq, Giza, on Wednesday, killing at least 31 and injuring 21. Twenty-seven vehicles were destroyed; 39 ambulances were sent.

5
ASIA  ·  PYONGYANG

KIM CALLS FOR MORE TACTICAL NUCLEAR WEAPONS

Kim Jong Un called for a wider and more diversified deployment of tactical nuclear weapons, state media reported. North Korea also expelled the head of the Swiss diplomatic office over sanctions.

6
SPACE  ·  WASHINGTON

FCC CLEARS SPACEX ORBITAL DATA CENTERS

The US Federal Communications Commission granted SpaceX permission to deploy a million data centers beyond Earth — orbital data centers in space.

7
WAR  ·  RUSSIA

UKRAINE HITS RUSSIAN AIR BASE, FIVE PLANES DAMAGED

Ukraine said it struck a major Russian air base, damaging five planes, in the latest long-range strike of the war.

8
MUSIC  ·  UNITED KINGDOM

ANDY TAYLOR DIES AT 65

Duran Duran guitarist and songwriter Andy Taylor has died at 65.

9
LAW  ·  WASHINGTON

BIRTHRIGHT CITIZENSHIP CURB BLOCKED AGAIN

President Trump's newest order curtailing birthright citizenship was blocked by a second judge.

10
MOURNING  ·  HONG KONG

HONG KONG MOURNS 168 KILLED IN HOUSING FIRE

Hong Kong remembered the victims of a housing complex fire that killed 168 people, as the city mourned one of its deadliest fires.

11
PAGEANT  ·  PUERTO RICO

FIRST MARRIED MISS UNIVERSE CROWNED

Jennifer Barreto of Puerto Rico was named Miss Universe 2026 — the first married contestant to take the title — with the pageant held in her homeland.
